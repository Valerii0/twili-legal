5. Manage, switch, or cancel

You can manage or cancel through Twili’s “Manage Subscription” link, through Apple’s subscription management page, or on iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select Twili.
  4. Choose another available plan or tap Cancel Subscription.

Plan changes are handled by Apple. Apple determines when a switch takes effect and whether a prorated credit, refund, or new charge applies, and displays the relevant information before confirmation.

Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the already-paid period, so you may retain paid access until the expiration date shown by Apple unless the transaction is refunded, revoked, or applicable law requires a different result. Cancelling stops future renewal; it does not by itself refund the current period.

Uninstalling Twili, deleting an account or in-app data, or simply stopping use does not cancel Apple billing. You must cancel through Apple. If you also want to stop future charges after requesting data or account deletion, cancel the subscription separately through Apple.
