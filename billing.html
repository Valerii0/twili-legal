1. Plans and purchases

These terms apply to subscriptions purchased in Twili through Apple’s App Store and supplement our Terms of Use. The operator is Valerii Petrychenko, a sole proprietor based in Kyiv, Ukraine.

Before you purchase, Twili and Apple show the available plan, included features, price, billing period and any offer. Confirming the purchase with Apple authorizes the displayed charge. The purchaser must be an adult authorized to use the Apple Account and payment method.

Prices and offers may vary by country and eligibility. Apple’s purchase confirmation shows the applicable currency and billing terms. Any price expressed as a weekly or monthly equivalent is a comparison; you are charged for the full billing period you select. Apple handles payment and applicable taxes under its storefront rules.

2. Free trials and introductory offers

A trial or introductory offer applies only if it is displayed in Apple’s purchase confirmation. That screen states the offer duration, any initial charge, and the subscription price that follows. If no offer is displayed, the regular price applies from purchase.

Unless canceled, an offer converts to the displayed paid subscription when the offer period ends. To avoid a charge after a trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Eligibility and availability are determined under the offer conditions and Apple’s rules.

3. Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same billing period unless canceled. Apple may charge for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before renewal and check the status in your Apple subscription settings.

If payment fails, Apple may retry the charge. Paid access may end while payment remains unresolved; any grace period depends on Apple and the applicable subscription settings.

4. Manage or cancel

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Twili to change your plan or cancel. You can also open Apple’s subscription management page.

Cancellation stops future renewal and normally leaves paid access available until the end of the paid period. Trial access may end earlier when canceled. Apple displays the applicable expiry date and the timing and charges for a plan change.

Deleting Twili, deleting your data, contacting support or stopping use does not cancel a subscription. Cancellation also does not automatically refund an existing charge.

5. Paid access and usage limits

An active subscription unlocks the paid features described when you purchase. Any generation allowance or other usage limit disclosed with the plan forms part of your purchase. A subscription does not promise unlimited generation unless expressly stated. Reasonable safety and abuse-prevention restrictions also apply.

We will not reduce a disclosed paid allowance during its purchased billing period except where necessary for safety, security, abuse prevention or legal compliance. If a paid service is materially discontinued, we will provide any notice and remedy required by law.

Paid features may become unavailable when a subscription expires or a transaction is refunded or revoked. Stories already saved on your device remain available to read. Family Sharing is available only if the selected subscription is identified as eligible by Apple.

6. Restore purchases

If an active subscription is not recognized, sign in with the Apple Account used to purchase it and choose Restore Purchases in Twili. Restoration does not create a new charge. It restores eligible paid access, not a deleted story library. Contact us if Apple confirms your subscription is active but access is still missing.

7. Refunds and consumer rights

To request a refund for an App Store purchase, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles refund requests under its policies and applicable law. Twili support can help with app access but does not control Apple’s refund decision or processing time.

Nothing in these terms limits mandatory refund, withdrawal, cancellation or other consumer rights. Your purchase is also subject to the Apple Media Services Terms for your country or region. Those terms govern the billing matters Apple administers, subject to mandatory law.

8. Changes

Price and plan changes apply prospectively, with notice and consent where required by Apple’s rules or applicable law. Apple may request approval of a new renewal price; if required approval is not given, the subscription may expire.

We will update the date above when these terms change and provide additional notice where required for a material change. Changes do not retroactively alter completed charges or reduce mandatory consumer rights.

9. Contact

For subscription access or other Twili support, use Contact Us in the app or email twili.support@gmail.com. Include only the details needed to investigate; never send your Apple Account password or full payment-card details.
