1. Who we are

Twili is operated by Valerii Petrychenko, a sole proprietor based in Kyiv, Ukraine (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We are responsible for personal information processed through the Twili app and related services. Our contact details are below.

2. Information we use

We receive information you provide, information collected when you use Twili, and purchase information from Apple and our service providers. We use it as follows:

  • Profiles and story content. A child’s name or nickname and age, your story instructions and requested length are processed online to generate personalized stories and illustrations. These inputs are also used for product analytics and service improvement. The profile’s gender selection and optional photo stay on your device, subject to your device backups.
  • Contact information. Your email address, messages and related request details let us handle support, feedback and privacy requests.
  • Usage and technical information. App and device identifiers, device and app information, IP address, approximate region, interactions, feature preferences, errors and diagnostics help us recognize your installation, provide and secure the service, understand use, and improve performance and features.
  • Purchase information. Transaction records, selected offers and subscription status let us provide, verify and restore paid access. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your full payment-card details.

Saved profiles, stories and settings are stored on your device and may be included in backups you enable.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not use AI to make decisions about you or a child that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Who receives information

We use service providers for hosting and storage, AI content generation, analytics, diagnostics, customer communications and subscription management. They receive the information needed for these functions, subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and data-protection obligations. Story inputs and generated content are processed by our AI providers; the other uses are described above.

Apple processes App Store purchases and any device backups under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share a story, the recipients and services you select receive that content.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect people or enforce legal rights, or as part of a transfer of our business, subject to applicable privacy protections.

4. Children’s information

Twili is designed for adults to create stories for children. An adult must operate the app and be the child’s parent, legal guardian or otherwise legally authorized to provide the child’s information. Children must not independently submit personal information or make purchases.

Use a nickname where possible and provide only what is needed for a story. Avoid sensitive information or details that could identify or locate a child outside your household.

A parent or guardian may request access to, correction or deletion of a child’s information, or ask us to stop its further collection. If we learn that a child submitted information without required authorization, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

5. Legal grounds

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process adult users’ information to provide the service under our agreement with them. We also process information to meet legal obligations and for our legitimate interests in providing requested personalization, supporting users, protecting the service and improving it, balanced against the rights of affected adults and children. We rely on consent where required by law.

Providing personal information is not a statutory requirement, but we may be unable to deliver a requested feature or respond to a request without the information needed for it.

6. Retention and deletion

We automatically delete your Twili service account after six months of inactivity. You may also request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time using the contact details below.

Other personal information is retained only for as long as needed for its stated purpose. We consider whether it is needed to complete or recover a story request, investigate errors, understand product use, resolve a support request, or meet security, transaction-record and legal obligations. We then delete or anonymize it, subject to backup cycles. Limited records may be retained where legally permitted and necessary, including for fraud prevention, accounting or legal claims.

Deleting a child profile removes that profile and its saved stories from the app. This does not automatically delete information already processed online or copies in device backups. Manage device backups through your device settings.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or a portable copy of your information; object to or restrict processing; and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Parents and guardians may exercise applicable rights for a child. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Tell us which right you wish to exercise and provide only the information needed to locate the relevant records. We may verify your identity and, where relevant, your authority to act for a child or another person. We respond within the time required by law and do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If a right to appeal applies, you may ask us to review a refusal through the same contact channels.

For choices not available in the app, including requests to stop analytics processing, contact us. Uninstalling the app stops new collection from that installation but does not itself delete existing records or cancel a subscription; see the Subscription Terms.

8. International processing and security

We operate from Ukraine, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Privacy laws may differ from those where you live. Where required, transfers must be covered by an applicable adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism. You may contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update the date above when this policy changes and provide additional notice or request consent where required for a material change.

10. Contact

For support or privacy requests, including requests about a child’s information, use Contact Us in the app or email twili.support@gmail.com.
