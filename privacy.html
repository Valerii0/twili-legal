2. Plain-language summary

No named Twili login

The app silently creates a random installation identifier, cryptographic credentials, and session tokens so the backend can recognize the installation.

Profiles and saved stories are mainly local

Names, ages, gender selections, optional photos, saved story text, titles, images, and favorites are stored on the device and may enter Apple device backups.

Generation leaves the device

Child name or nickname, age, story theme, and requested length go to Twili’s Railway-hosted backend and onward to OpenAI’s API platform. Generated text, title, and image return through that backend.

Analytics includes child inputs

Amplitude receives child name or nickname, exact age, theme, and story length when generation starts.

In-app Contact Us uses Amplitude

Pressing Send attempts to submit the email address and full message through Amplitude as Twili's in-app support, feedback, and privacy-request channel. The app does not receive an independent delivery acknowledgement; use email for a time-sensitive request.

No payment-card access

Apple handles payment. RevenueCat receives the random installation ID and subscription/entitlement records needed to provide premium access.

Twili does not include third-party advertising, and we do not use information for cross-app behavioral advertising. Twili does not upload the optional child profile photo as part of a story-generation request.

5. Children and parental controls

Twili creates content for families but is designed to be installed, configured, and operated only by a parent or legal guardian who is at least 18. Children, including teenagers, may read or listen to adult-reviewed stories and suggest ideas while an adult operates the app, but they must not independently create a profile, type or submit a theme, make a purchase, use Contact us, manage settings, or otherwise submit personal information. Twili does not provide a public child profile, social feed, or child-to-child messaging.

The adult represents that they are the child’s parent or legal guardian and have authority to provide information about the child and request its processing. For a real child who is old enough for the generation flow described below, use a nickname or first name rather than a full legal name, provide only the age needed for personalization, avoid sensitive themes, and omit the optional photo unless it is genuinely useful. The profile photo remains local in the generation flow, subject to device backups.

We treat child-profile information as child data even when an adult supplies it. Applicable children's privacy law may give a parent the right to review, delete, or stop further collection of a child's information and may require deletion if a child submitted it directly without the necessary authorization or verifiable parental consent. A parent may submit a request at any time through Contact Us in the app or by emailing twili.support@gmail.com. We may verify the requester's identity and authority before acting. A request should identify the affected Twili installation and child profile without including unnecessary child data.

We do not knowingly sell child data, use it for targeted advertising, or condition a child's participation on providing more online information than reasonably necessary for the requested story. We disclose child name or nickname and age to the generation and analytics providers listed in Section 6; those disclosures are not public. The app does not provide a child-facing verifiable-consent flow, so an adult must remain the user of all data-entry features.

Twili's OpenAI configuration does not include Zero Data Retention. An adult must not use AI generation with a profile or information that represents, identifies, or could reasonably be linked to a real child under 13, or under the applicable age of digital consent where the child lives. For a story intended for a younger audience, use a wholly fictional character and fictional age setting that are not linked to a real child. Do not submit the real child’s name, nickname, exact age, address, school, contact information, health information, photograph, or any other personal or sensitive information.

7. AI processing

The iOS app does not connect directly to OpenAI. It sends the selected child name or nickname, age, free-text theme, and story length to Twili's backend. After server-side authentication, subscription validation, and request processing, the backend sends the information required for generation to OpenAI's API. Twili uses OpenAI API models for text generation through POST /v1/responses and image generation through POST /v1/images/generations. Model versions may change as the service evolves. Generated text and imagery return through Twili's backend and may be saved on the device.

Twili sends Responses API requests with store: false and does not use stored response IDs, previous_response_id, or OpenAI continuation features. This means Twili does not ask OpenAI to store a Responses API response object for later API use; it does not mean that Zero Data Retention applies. Twili has not enabled Zero Data Retention or Modified Abuse Monitoring and has not selected a special OpenAI processing or data-residency region. OpenAI may process information in accordance with its own API data-processing and retention practices, including standard abuse-monitoring practices described in Section 6.

OpenAI's under-18 API guidance addresses additional safeguards for services used in connection with minors. Because Twili does not use Zero Data Retention, the restrictions in Section 5 apply to information concerning children under 13 or the applicable age of digital consent. AI output can be inaccurate, unsuitable, or unexpected, and an adult must review each story and image before sharing it with a child. Twili does not use AI to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about an adult or child.

8. Retention, local deletion, and remote deletion

Record Retention approach
Local child profiles and stories Kept on the device until the adult deletes the child profile (which deletes that child’s saved stories), the data is otherwise removed in-app, or the app is uninstalled. Copies may remain in Apple device backups until the user deletes or Apple rotates those backups.
Local UUID, tokens, and preferences Kept in the app’s local settings container while the app is installed or until reset/deletion. Uninstalling normally removes the active local copy, but does not by itself delete server/provider records or backups.
Twili backend story prompts and generated output Story prompts and generated output temporarily stored in Twili-controlled systems hosted on Railway are retained for approximately three days. A cleanup job runs once per day and deletes story content older than three days on its next run, so deletion does not necessarily occur exactly 72 hours after collection. This schedule applies only to story content on Twili's backend and does not govern OpenAI, Amplitude, Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple, backups, or other provider records.
Backend account/profile and operational records The pseudonymous Twili account/profile is automatically deleted after more than six months without activity. It may be deleted sooner following a verified request. Authentication, quota, security, fraud-prevention, network, and backup records are not covered by the three-day story-content cleanup and may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, fraud prevention, backup rotation, legal compliance, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of claims.
Amplitude analytics and Contact Us submissions Kept according to applicable Amplitude retention practices and Twili's project settings. Twili does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period for Amplitude records. The Twili/Railway story-content cleanup does not delete Amplitude events. Twili can use the internal user/account identifier to locate user-linked Amplitude data and manually delete it or submit it for deletion following a verified privacy request.
OpenAI prompts and outputs Subject to the OpenAI account and endpoint controls described in Sections 6 and 7. Under standard controls, abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, and endpoint-specific application state may also apply. Twili’s three-day backend cleanup does not control OpenAI’s retention.
Crash, configuration, and subscription records Kept for the period needed to diagnose supported releases, deliver configuration or entitlements, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and meet accounting or legal obligations, subject to Google, RevenueCat, and Apple retention practices. Historical Crashlytics records may not be physically deleted immediately. Following a verified request, we delete or request deletion of associated data from these providers where supported.
Privacy requests and legal records Kept as needed to verify and document compliance, prevent fraudulent requests, enforce agreements, and meet limitation periods or legal duties.

What an in-app deletion does—and does not do

Choosing Delete child removes that profile and every locally saved story associated with it from the active app container. It does not automatically erase earlier generation requests, Amplitude events, Crashlytics records, RevenueCat records, backend logs, AI-provider copies, or existing Apple backups because those systems are separate. Uninstalling the app has the same limitation.

An adult may request deletion of the Twili account and associated personal information at any time through Contact Us in the app or by emailing twili.support@gmail.com. After verifying the request, we delete the account/profile and associated server-side data and delete or request deletion of associated data from our service providers where supported. Provider systems, backups, security records, and legally required records may not be deleted immediately or may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, transactions, accounting, disputes, or other legitimate purposes permitted by law.

Deleting Twili data does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancellation is governed by the Subscription & Billing Terms.

Requests are ordinarily free, but we may charge or decline where the law permits for manifestly unfounded, excessive, repetitive, or unverifiable requests. You will not be discriminated against for exercising a privacy right. If applicable law provides an appeal right and we deny the request, our response will explain how to appeal.

You may delete local profiles and stories using the app's controls. The app does not provide an in-app switch to disable Amplitude or Crashlytics collection; uninstalling the app stops future collection from that installation. You may contact us to object to processing or request deletion of user-linked provider data. We will honor the request to the extent required by applicable law and supported by the relevant provider. Device backup choices are managed in Apple settings, and subscription choices are managed through Apple.
